WhatsApp Business API Platform for Teams | Convora

Convora is a WhatsApp Business API platform built for sales, support and marketing teams that want to turn conversations into revenue. Run broadcast campaigns, manage approved message templates, reply from a shared team inbox, and track delivery analytics in one dashboard — all on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.

Unlike unofficial bulk-sender tools, Convora connects to Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform so your number keeps a verified business profile, quality rating visibility, and compliant messaging limits. Teams across India use Convora to launch offers, follow up leads, send order updates, and support customers where they already chat every day: WhatsApp.

Why teams choose this WhatsApp Business API platform

Growing businesses need more than a personal WhatsApp handset. They need a platform that scales broadcasts safely, keeps history visible to the whole team, and shows whether each message was sent, delivered, read or failed. Convora gives product, growth and support leads that operational clarity without forcing them to stitch together multiple tools.
